# ChatGrow
### Your WhatsApp. Your Team. Your CRM.

ChatGrow helps businesses organize customer conversations, manage leads and coordinate sales and support on WhatsApp.

- Shared team inbox and customer context
- Lead management and sales pipelines
- Chatbot flows and follow-up automation
- Targeted campaigns and customer engagement
